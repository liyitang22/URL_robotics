01 / PRELIMINARIES

Forward-Backward Representations

Start here: the foundational forward-backward representation paper.

Learning One Representation to Optimize All Rewards

NeurIPS 2021 · Ahmed Touati, Yann Ollivier

Introduces forward-backward representations for reward-free dynamics, enabling policies for many rewards to be derived from a shared learned representation.

Representation Improvements & Variants

Soft Forward-Backward Representations for Zero-shot Reinforcement Learning with General Utilities

Marco Bagatella, Thomas Rupf, Georg Martius, Andreas Krause · 2026

Extends forward-backward methods beyond additive reward objectives to broader utility functions, using a soft maximum-entropy formulation.

Paper

Regularized Latent Dynamics Prediction is a Strong Baseline For Behavioral Foundation Models

Pranaya Jajoo, Harshit Sikchi, Siddhant Agarwal, Amy Zhang, Scott Niekum, Martha White · 2026

Asks whether complex representation objectives are necessary for behavioral foundation models, and shows that regularized latent next-state prediction is a strong alternative baseline.

Paper

Zero-Shot Off-Policy Learning

Arip Asadulaev, Maksim Bobrin, Salem Lahlou, Dmitry Dylov, Fakhri Karray, Martin Takac · 2026

Studies zero-shot off-policy adaptation through successor measures and stationary density correction, connecting naturally to forward-backward representations.

Paper

Can We Really Learn One Representation to Optimize All Rewards?

Chongyi Zheng, Royina Karegoudra Jayanth, Benjamin Eysenbach · 2026

Analyzes when forward-backward representations exist and how their low-rank successor-measure approximation converges, then proposes a simpler, more tractable FB variant.
